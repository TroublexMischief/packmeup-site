PackMeUp Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

PackMeUp is a packing list app for iPhone. It does not collect, store or share any personal data. This page explains what that means in practice.

What the app stores

Your trips, packing lists, traveller names and Item Library are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You do not need an account, and the app works fully offline.

What we collect

Nothing. PackMeUp contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting code, and it makes no network requests.

Sharing and backups

When you send a list or export your data, PackMeUp creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as a message or your Files app. A shared list includes your Item Library and any traveller names on the list.

If you use iCloud Backup or move to a new iPhone with Quick Start, your PackMeUp data is included the same way as other app data. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy.

Children

PackMeUp collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
