PackMeUp Support

Help with the PackMeUp packing list app for iPhone

Contact

Email noora@jarvi.fi and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I make a trip?
Tap Create on the Trips screen and give the trip a name. Your list is ready straight away. Open Trip details to add the number of days, travellers and trip types.

Why don't I see quantities?
Quantities appear once the trip has a length. Open Trip details and set the number of days.

How do I change the items every new trip starts with?
Tap the gear icon and open Item Library. You can add, edit, delete and reorder items there.

How do I share a list with someone?
Open the trip and choose Send list from its menu. The other person opens the .packmeup file in PackMeUp and gets their own copy. Later changes are not synced.

How do I move my trips to a new iPhone?
iCloud Backup and Quick Start move PackMeUp along with your other apps. You can also use Settings › Export all data on the old phone and choose Settings › Import backup on the new one.

Do I need an account?
No. PackMeUp keeps everything on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
